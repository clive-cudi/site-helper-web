## Common Customer Questions (FAQ)

### 1) How do I know if an item is in stock?
If the "Add to Cart" button is active, the item is in stock. If a size is sold out, customers can select "Notify Me" on the product page.

### 2) Can I change or cancel my order?
Orders can be changed or canceled within 1 hour of placement by contacting support. After fulfillment starts, changes may not be possible.

### 3) Do you offer price matching?
We offer price matching on identical in-stock items from approved retailers within 7 days of purchase. Exclusions apply to clearance and flash-sale items.

### 4) Do you have student or military discounts?
Yes. Verified students and military members receive 10% off eligible full-price items.

### 5) What payment methods do you accept?
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

### 6) Is local pickup available?
Yes. Select "Store Pickup" at checkout. Pickup confirmation is sent by email when the order is ready.
